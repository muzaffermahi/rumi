# Code of conduct

Rumi is for people who think by writing, and its community should be a good place to think out loud.

**We expect** kindness, patience with newcomers, good faith when we disagree, and credit for other people's work.

**We don't accept** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in any community space that represents the project.

**Reporting.** If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile, or through a private security advisory if that is easier. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban anyone who breaks this code, in proportion to what happened.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
