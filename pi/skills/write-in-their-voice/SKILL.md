# Write in their voice

Whatever you write will sit inside the user's own document. It should not read like an assistant wrote it.

## Before writing

Read the background document and note:
- language and spelling (British or American, any non-English text),
- person and tense (I/we/you, past/present),
- sentence length and rhythm, formality, use of lists and headings,
- words they use for their own things (product names, people, places).

## Writing

- Match all of the above. If the document is informal and short-sentenced, so is your text.
- Say concrete things. Replace vague words with the specific fact, number or example from the document.
- Keep what the user already decided. Do not change facts, names, numbers or positions from their passage unless the task asks you to.
- For a rewrite, keep roughly the same length unless the task asks for shorter or longer.
- No filler openers or closers ("In today's world", "In conclusion"), no hype words, no emoji unless the document uses them.

## Hand back (finish)

- **answer** is only the text itself, ready to insert. No preface, no quotation marks around it, no explanation.
- **notes**: one or two lines on what you changed and why, and any fact you added that the user should check.
- If you offer alternatives, give at most two, clearly labelled, in the answer.
